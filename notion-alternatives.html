Buyer Question Answered

"What should I switch to from Notion if I want notes, wikis and tasks without the Plus price or cloud lock-in?"

Compares five Notion alternatives on price, offline use and how hard migration is. Notion Plus adds up once a team grows, and some people want local-first files or a simpler wiki. How to switch: export Notion pages as Markdown/CSV, pick a cutover weekend, import into the new tool (or keep Markdown in a folder), run both for two weeks, then cancel Plus before renewal.
